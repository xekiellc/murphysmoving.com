Questions

GOOD TO KNOW

How much does junk removal cost?

By the load: $135 for one pickup truck load, $265 for a quarter trailer, $450 for a half, $700 for a full load. See pricing above for the breakdown.

Do you do full moves, not just junk?

Yes — residential, apartment, and small office moves, plus storage and POD loading. Every home is different, so moving jobs are quoted with a free estimate rather than a flat rate.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes — free estimate, no obligation.

Can you help with donations or recycling, not just landfill?

Yes — donation pickup and recycling are part of what we offer alongside standard removal.

What if my item or job doesn't fit neatly into a load size?

Every job's a little different — call or text and we'll work out what it actually needs.

How far in advance should I book?

Give us a call or text and we'll tell you what's open — we'd rather answer that live than guess here.
